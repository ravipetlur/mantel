The first public build of Mantel.

- **Hide icons.** Sort apps into Shown, Hidden and Always Hidden, and click the • dot to see the hidden ones.
- **A second bar.** Hidden icons that don't fit open in a small bar under the dot. Click one and its own menu opens.
- **Arrange the menu bar.** Drag icons in Settings to reorder the real menu bar, and add spacers, groups and a divider.
- **Find any icon.** A command bar finds an icon by name and clicks it, and any icon can have its own hotkey.

Needs macOS 27 on Apple Silicon. Mantel is early: expect rough edges.
