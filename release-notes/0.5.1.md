- **Calls no longer bring your hidden icons out.** To keep the mic and camera dots visible, 0.5.0 switched to hiding icons with System Settings' "Allow in the Menu Bar" switches. On this version of macOS, those switches don't hide icons when Mantel changes them, so every hidden icon showed during a call. Mantel no longer uses that way of hiding. In Settings › Advanced, the option and the mic and camera switch are dimmed, with the reason. During calls the dots are hidden again, as before 0.5.0.
